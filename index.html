03 · How I work

Simple, structured, and collaborative.

Whether it's a company landing page or a Flutter screen, I follow the same three steps.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Listen first. Understand the product, the users' pain points, and what isn't working in the current experience.

  2. 02

    Design

    Map out a clean layout in Figma or Canva with clear hierarchy, readable type, and mobile-first responsiveness.

  3. 03

    Deliver & iterate

    Build it in HTML, CSS, JS, Tailwind, or Flutter, test across screen sizes, and keep improving with team feedback.
