03 / How I Work

Simple, structured, and collaborative.

Whether I'm revamping a company landing page or designing a mobile screen in Flutter, I follow a clear 3-step workflow.

01
🔍

Step 1: Discovery

I start by listening and understanding the product, the users' pain points, and what isn't working in the current experience—just like diagnosing why an old website feels confusing.

02
🎨

Step 2: Design

Using Figma and Canva, I map out a clean, distraction-free layout with strong visual hierarchy, readable typography, and mobile-first responsiveness.

03
🚀

Step 3: Deliver & Iterate

I translate the design into clean code (HTML, CSS, JS, Tailwind, or Flutter), test across screen sizes, and stay open to team feedback to keep improving the final result.
